## Animals without a recording

No clearly free (CC0, public-domain or CC BY) recording was found for these. The game plays a short synthesised Web Audio sound (or none) and then speaks the animal's name:

- rabbit, hamster, fish, tortoise (pets)
- giraffe, zebra, hippo, penguin (zoo)

The trimmed files were produced from the originals linked above; all edits (trim, fade, gain, MP3 encoding) are released under the same terms as the originals.
